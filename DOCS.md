# Fresh Fruit Daily — Official Product & Subscription Guide

Welcome to the **Fresh Fruit Daily** documentation. This guide covers box options, ordering workflows, storage tips, and delivery policies.

## 1. Available Fruit Boxes
- **Daily Wellness Box:** Single-serve portions of 4 seasonal fruits ideal for workdays and smoothies.
- **Family Harvest Box:** 5 kg assortment of everyday staples (bananas, apples, papayas, pomegranates) plus 2 seasonal specialties.
- **Exotic & Tropical Box:** Curated dragon fruit, avocados, kiwis, Alphonso mangoes (seasonal), and berries.

## 2. Practical Workflow: How to Customize & Pause Your Subscription
1. **Select Your Base Plan:** Choose between Weekly (3 deliveries) or Daily (6 deliveries/week) on our website.
2. **Swap Seasonal Items:** Up to 12 hours before dispatch, update your disliked items list so our packers substitute your preferred fruits.
3. **One-Click Vacation Pause:** Going out of town? Pause deliveries anytime with zero cancellation fees and carry credits forward.

## 3. Storage & Freshness Tips
- Keep **bananas, mangoes, and papayas** at room temperature until ripe, then refrigerate.
- Store **berries and grapes** unwashed in breathable containers in the crisper drawer.
